Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Reelab is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. The owner is a wedding DJ in Egypt. Reelab edits short vertical videos from the owner's own wedding footage and, only after the owner approves each one, publishes them to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

How it works

  1. The owner puts footage from his weddings in a folder on his computer.
  2. Reelab edits short vertical videos and writes captions.
  3. Each video waits for the owner's approval. He watches it, chooses where it goes and when, and approves or rejects it.
  4. Approved videos are uploaded through each platform's official API (for TikTok: Login Kit and the Content Posting API).

Reelab is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.
