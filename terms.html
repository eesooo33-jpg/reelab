ReelabPrivacy Policy

Reelab is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It edits short vertical videos from the owner's own footage and, only after the owner approves each post, publishes them to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Who can use it

Reelab is used only by its owner, to manage the owner's own social media accounts.

Your content

You are responsible for the videos, music and captions you publish with Reelab, and for having the rights to use them. Reelab never publishes anything without your explicit approval of that specific post.

Platform rules

When you connect TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, you remain bound by that platform's own terms and community guidelines. You can disconnect any account at any time from Reelab's settings or from the platform's own settings.

No warranty

Reelab is provided as is. Posting can fail if a platform rejects a video or changes its service; Reelab shows these errors so you can retry.
