Reelab Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

ReelabTerms of Service

What Reelab accesses

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account is connected, and permission to upload and publish videos you approve (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram and YouTube: permission to publish videos you approve, and audience activity times used to suggest good posting hours.
  • The video, photo and music files in the folders you choose on your computer.

Where data is stored

Everything stays on your computer: access tokens, settings, edited videos and the posting schedule are stored in Reelab's local folder. Reelab has no server and no database outside your computer.

What is shared

Reelab sends data only to the platform you choose to publish on: the approved video and its caption go to that platform through its official API. Reelab does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Deleting your data

Disconnect an account in Reelab's settings to delete its token from your computer, or revoke Reelab's access from the platform's own app settings. Deleting Reelab's folder removes all of its data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner of the connected TikTok account through a direct message on TikTok.
